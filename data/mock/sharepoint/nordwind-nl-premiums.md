# Nordwind Retail – Sunday & overtime premiums

Applies to: **Nordwind Retail Nederland B.V.** (CAO Retail Non-Food).

- Sunday work (zondagtoeslag): **50% premium** on the hourly wage.
- Overtime (overuren) above 40h/week: 25% premium, or time off in lieu.

Verified by Daan Visser (Payroll NL) on 02/07/2026.
