# Nordwind Retail BE – Overtime & Sunday work rules

Applies to: Nordwind Retail NV (Belgium), store and warehouse staff, JC 311.

## Overtime
- Weekday overtime (above 38h/week): **50% premium** on top of the hourly wage.
- Overtime can be recovered as time off instead, on request of the employee.

## Sunday work
- Overtime hours worked on a Sunday are paid with a **100% premium** on top of the hourly wage.
- Sunday hours must be registered in the time module with code SUN-OT.
