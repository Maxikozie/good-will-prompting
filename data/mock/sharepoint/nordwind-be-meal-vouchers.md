# Nordwind Retail BE – Meal vouchers

- Nordwind Retail BE grants a meal voucher of **€8.00 per worked day**.
- Employer contribution: €6.91, employee contribution: €1.09.
- Vouchers are loaded electronically on the first working day of the month.
