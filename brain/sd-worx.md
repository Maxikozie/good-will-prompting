## Who has the problem
Internal SD Worx staff, not the end customer:
- **Customer service / support colleagues** answering urgent employee questions for client companies.
- **Payroll consultants** managing client portfolios across countries and teams.

## Where knowledge lives today
Documents · manuals · checklists · SharePoint · MS Teams channels and chats · emails · people's heads. They already have an AI assistant that retrieves documents, and that's part of the problem: it returns sources without telling you which to trust.

## Useful domain terms
- **Payslip**: the monthly document showing gross wage, net wage, deductions.
- **Payroll engine**: the system that calculates pay. SD Worx runs ~20 of its own, often per country, which is why a rule can differ by country.
- **HCM**: Human Capital Management. **WFM**: Workforce Management.
